About REVMAXX

Established in June 2025, REVMAXX was founded to deliver premium automotive importing and brokerage services across Sri Lanka. We bridge the gap between elite global auctions and local car enthusiasts, ensuring a transparent process built on absolute trust.

From sourcing high-performance vehicles to handling secure international shipping and local customs, we provide the expertise needed to import your next car with complete confidence.

The Acquisition Process

Five stages, from first enquiry to a vehicle in your possession — managed end-to-end by the broker desk.

01

Enquiry

Submit your spec through the private sourcing desk. A broker reviews it within hours.

02

Global Sourcing

We canvas private collector networks and international auction houses for a matching vehicle.

03

Verification

Every vehicle is physically inspected and condition-reported before any commitment is made.

04

Import & Customs

Fully insured shipping, Colombo Port clearance, and duty handling — managed end-to-end.

05

Delivery

Your vehicle arrives ready to register and drive, with full documentation in hand.
